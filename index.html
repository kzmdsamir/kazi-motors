Sourced the Dhaka way. Most reconditioned parts in Bangladesh flow through Dholaikhal in Old Dhaka — thousands of specialist shops dealing in everything from nuts and bolts to complete engines, fed by imports through Chattogram port and stock from Japan, Singapore, the UAE and beyond. Many parts for older Toyotas are no longer manufactured at all, which is why this trade keeps Dhaka moving.

Kazi Motors works that same supply chain, then adds the part most shops skip: a straight answer. If a part is worn, repaired or the wrong match for your chassis, we'd rather tell you than sell it to you.
